Pandemic Stimulus — Typical household gain, in local currency

All figures in local currency. Each row shows what the typical (median) household within that wealth tier gained, corrected for the skew within each tier. The "years of median income" column shows the gain as a multiple of the national median annual income. The bar charts use actual amounts, with no log scale. The ordering of gains across tiers follows from the lending formula; the dollar magnitudes depend on two estimates explained below.
