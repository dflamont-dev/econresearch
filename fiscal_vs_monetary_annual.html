Fiscal vs Monetary Stimulus — Annual estimates, March 2020 to March 2024

All figures in USD billions at approximate average annual exchange rates. Fiscal stimulus = direct government pandemic expenditure (household payments, wage subsidies, business loans, tax provisions). Monetary stimulus wealth effect = estimated household wealth gains attributable to central bank rate cuts and QE (asset price revaluation channel), net of partial 2022–23 reversals. Monetary figures dwarf fiscal in every year except 2020, when fiscal transfers were largest and asset markets had not yet fully recovered.

Sources & methodology. Fiscal totals: Canada C$346B (Bank of Canada 2024, "Review of Exceptional Policy Actions During the Pandemic," Box 1); US $5.3T (CARES Act, ARP, and associated legislation); UK £400B (HM Treasury COVID-19 accounts); Australia A$280B (Australian Government COVID-19 response); New Zealand NZ$90B (NZ Treasury COVID-19 accounts). Annual breakdown estimated from published programme timelines; 2020 is heavily front-loaded in all countries. Monetary wealth effect totals: derived from national accounts (Fed DFA, Stats Canada DNBS, ABS Household Financial Accounts, ONS/Resolution Foundation, Stats NZ), attributing approximately 65–70% of total household wealth gains to monetary policy (asset price revaluation channel), following Batty, Deeken & Volz (2021). 2022 figures reflect partial reversal from rate hikes; 2023 shows modest recovery. Net four-year totals may differ from the main table due to rounding and the treatment of 2022 reversals. Exchange rates: CAD/USD 0.785, GBP/USD 1.30, AUD/USD 0.755, NZD/USD 0.700 (approximate 2021 averages used throughout for consistency).
