Model assumptions: Fixed monthly payment $2,000 over 30 years. Credit spreads above base rate by tier: Elite +1.25%, Prime +2.5%, Standard +4.5%, Subprime +7.5%, Marginal +12.0%. Spread structure is illustrative of the Canadian/US mortgage and consumer credit market. Population shares follow an 80/20 Pareto distribution mapped to creditworthiness. Wealth shares derived from 80/20 recursive Pareto: top 5% = ~51%, next 15% = ~24%, next 30% = ~15%, next 25% = ~7%, bottom 25% = ~3%. Amplification gap = difference in max loan vs benchmark of 5.0% base rate.
