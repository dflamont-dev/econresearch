Income-scaled payments assume ~30% gross debt service ratio on estimated median annual incomes per stratum: Ultra-elite $1.6M/yr → $40,000/mo; Elite $320K/yr → $8,000/mo; Upper $160K/yr → $4,000/mo; Affluent $80K/yr → $2,000/mo; Majority $48K/yr → $1,200/mo. Credit spreads above base rate: Ultra-elite +0.50%, Elite +1.25%, Upper +2.50%, Affluent +4.00%, Majority +9.00% (weighted blend of standard, near-prime, subprime and non-qualifying borrowers). Amplification factor = ultra-elite dollar gain ÷ majority dollar gain vs 5% benchmark. At 0.25% base in income-scaled mode, ultra-elite gains ~$5.9M vs majority ~$45K ≈ 130×. Fixed-payment mode isolates the spread/rate mechanism alone (~3–4×); income-scaled mode adds payment capacity to reveal total structural amplification. Wealth strata from 80/20 Pareto: top 0.01% = 40% of property; 0.01–0.65% = 11%; 0.65–3.85% = 13%; 3.85–19.85% = 16%; bottom 80% = 20%.
